Type 5 · rotary table, 3 stations

Multi-station rotary

Three stations indexing 120°. One is moulded, one keeps cooling after injection and one is unloaded and loaded by the operator, for long cooling times or insert-heavy parts.

The cycle, step by step

How it works

Three stations sit on the turntable, which indexes 120° per cycle. At any moment one station is being moulded, one keeps cooling after injection and one is unloaded and loaded. The extra station suits long cooling times and parts that need many inserts.

  1. Clamp closes
  2. Injection unit moves down
  3. Injection
  4. Holding pressure & cooling
  5. Injection unit moves up
  6. Clamp opens
  7. Table rotates 120°
