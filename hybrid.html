Hybrid electro-hydraulic Hydraulic clamping – electric injection

Electric injection. Hydraulic clamping.

Part quality in insert moulding is decided in the injection unit: how exactly the screw doses and how precisely it injects and holds pressure. On the ET / ETU series that motion is servo-electric — controlled to 0.01 mm and repeatable within ±2 %.

The clamp stays hydraulic, driven by a servo pump (max. 140–175 kg/cm²) that only draws energy when it works. The result: the accuracy and low consumption of electric injection, with the clamping force, robustness and service-friendliness of a hydraulic vertical clamp.

Advantages

Six reasons to choose the hybrid series.

High precision

Servo-motor precision control reaches 0.01 mm, with a repeatability of ±2 %. Shot after shot, the part stays the same — the basis for precision overmoulding.

More efficient

Electric servo injection and a servo-driven hydraulic clamp: high efficiency and energy conversion, short cycles and low energy consumption.

More convenient

Ergonomic working conditions — the work table can be lowered to below 850 mm, so the operator loads inserts at a natural height.

High cost-effectiveness

Original German design and manufacturing process, technology and quality comparable with European machines — mass-produced, so the price stays competitive.

Smarter control

A more advanced control system with more powerful and accurate CPU control — 2 ms program scan and stable temperature control within ±0.5 °C.

More energy-efficient

Energy savings of 40–70 % compared with conventional hydraulic vertical machines.

Where it pays off

Parts that have to be right every shot.

  • Connectors & terminals — metal contacts overmoulded with tight positional tolerance.
  • Sensors & electronics — encapsulation of sensitive inserts at controlled pressure.
  • Precision metal overmoulding — bushings, pins and stampings in automotive parts.
  • Medical & optical parts — small shots where repeatability decides the scrap rate.

Smarter control system

  • Resistive colour touch screen
  • Intel processor — efficient, accurate CPU control with a 2 ms program scan cycle
  • Action sequence programmable online
  • Automatic adjustment of action slope (ramps)
  • Stable temperature control, ≤ ±0.5 °C
  • Remote fault diagnosis
  • Distributed control over the VARAN bus — easy module expansion
  • Any national language, with rich quality-management functions
