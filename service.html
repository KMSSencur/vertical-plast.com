Service & support

Support that keeps
the cell running.

A vertical injection moulding machine earns its place by running reliable insert and overmoulding cycles for years. KMS backs every machine with application engineering, parts and maintenance — locally, through a network reaching more than 30 countries.

Application engineering

We size the machine, table and automation to your insert and part before you buy — and stay on the process after commissioning.

Spare parts

Genuine wear and drive parts held for the full range, shipped to keep unplanned downtime short.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled servicing of clamp, table index and injection unit, with intervals set per model and duty cycle.

Commissioning & training

Installation, first-article support and operator training on the vertical rotary table and its cell.

Retrofit & automation

Add feeding, vision or take-out to an existing machine, or upgrade the table configuration.

Remote support

Diagnostics and guidance from an application engineer to resolve process issues quickly.

Global reach

Local support, worldwide.

Backed by three production bases and a capacity of 4,000 units per year, the platform behind KMS machines has served more than 5,000 customers and maintains agents across Europe, the Americas, Asia and Africa.
