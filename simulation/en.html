3R alternative · 3-station machine

The cell with the TY-2R machine was built in steps 1–7 (base proposal). For comparison we also propose a machine with a 3-station rotary table (TY-3R): at station 1 the small 6-axis robot NACHI MZ07L (or MZ10LF) first unloads the parts, then loads the first inserts with a single 1- or 2-nest gripper; at station 2 a SCARA loads the rest. The insert infeeds (each type its own), inspection and output remain as in the 2R cell.

Points — linking and symmetry

Anchors are corners, edge midpoints, the center and approach points — so the left and right sides are always symmetric. While dragging a point, hold Ctrl for free placement, and Alt to unlink it from the shape.
