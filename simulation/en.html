Layout

step [mm]
By default, placement is free (to the nearest millimeter). While dragging or dropping, hold Shift to temporarily invert the chosen setting.
Size: select an object and drag one of the white handles on its edge — or simply Ctrl + drag near the edge. For a robot, Ctrl + drag changes the reach. Minimum size is 100 mm.

nothing selected
Rotation turns the footprint and moves the robot entry point.

Points — linking and symmetry

Anchors are corners, edge midpoints, the center and approach points — so the left and right sides are always symmetric. While dragging a point, hold Ctrl for free placement, and Alt to unlink it from the shape.
