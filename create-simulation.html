Free online tool · English / slovenščina

Calculate clamping force and create automation.

Load your part and get the clamping force and the right TAYU machine — then build the automation cell around it and watch it run. You see the cycle time, the parts per shift and where the bottleneck is, and you can export the result as a PDF report.

  • Best on a desktop or laptop screen

How it works

  1. 1

    Calculate the clamping force

    Load a 3D model of your part (STL, OBJ, STEP or IGES) or use the example. Choose the orientation, material and cavities — you get the required clamping force, suitable TAYU machines and the cycle time.

  2. 2

    Build the cell

    Infeed, loading, inspection, unloading and output: the wizard builds a cell around the proposed TY-2R machine with your data already filled in — and suggests a 3-station TY-3R alternative to compare.

  3. 3

    Run it

    Press Run and the parts flow through the cell. Status lamps show every station; the analysis gives the cycle time, the output per shift and the bottleneck — export it as a PDF.

Want KMS to check your cell?

Send us the PDF report or the saved simulation file, together with your part — we propose the right TAYU machine and the automation around it.
