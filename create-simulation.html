Free online tool · English / slovenščina

Simulate your vertical moulding cell.

Pick a TAYU machine, set the cavities, insert loading and unloading — and watch your cell run. You see the cycle time, the parts per shift and where the bottleneck is, and you can export the result as a PDF report.

  • Best on a desktop or laptop screen

  • Your cell is saved on this device

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose the machine

    Standard table, single or double slide, or a rotary table with 2 or 3 stations — with the machine size, cavities and cycle.

  2. 2

    Build the cell

    Infeed, loading, inspection, unloading and output: the wizard places every unit on the layout — then drag, rotate and fine-tune them.

  3. 3

    Run it

    Press Run and the parts flow through the cell. Status lamps show every station; the analysis gives the cycle time, the output per shift and the bottleneck — export it as a PDF.

Want KMS to check your cell?

Send us the PDF report or the saved simulation file, together with your part — we propose the right TAYU machine and the automation around it.
