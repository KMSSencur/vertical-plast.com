Type 3 · Double slide

TY-DS series

Two lower moulds on a shuttle. One mould is formed while the other is taken out and loaded — then the table slides across. Loading time disappears from the cycle, and the operator always works outside the clamp.

  • 12–250 tclamping force
  • 28–828 gmax. shot weight (PS)
  • Ø20–Ø65screw options
  • 13machine sizes
Technical data Check available options

How it works

01

Double slide mode

Two lower moulds slide alternately: one moulds, the other is unloaded and loaded at the same time.

02

Higher output, same clamp

The clamp never waits for the operator — ideal for insert-heavy parts in series production.

03

Nitrided slide platens

Slide platens are nitrided for a longer service life.

04

High operating safety

Loading stations sit left and right of the clamp, outside the mould area.
